Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

Notes & Reminders ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled within the App.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The App is designed as a local-first utility. All notes, reminders, folders, and settings created within the App are stored exclusively on your device using a local database. We do not operate any external servers, and we do not collect, upload, or share your personal data with any third parties.

2. Local Storage and Backups

Your data remains under your full control. The App provides an "Export Backup" feature that allows you to create an encrypted file containing your notes and reminders. This file is saved to a location of your choice on your device or cloud storage. We do not have access to these files or the encryption keys used to protect them.

3. Permissions

To provide its core features, the App may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: Used solely to alert you of scheduled reminders.
  • Biometrics/Fingerprint: Used locally to verify your identity before revealing "Locked Notes." Your biometric data never leaves the secure hardware of your device and is never accessible by the App.
  • Storage/Files: Used only when you explicitly choose to Export or Import a backup file.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking software. Your usage of the App is completely private.

5. Security

The App includes features like Master PIN and Biometric locks to help you protect your sensitive notes locally. However, please remember that the overall security of your data depends on the security of your physical device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at nikhilcrlabs@gmail.com.
